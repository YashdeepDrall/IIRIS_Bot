# IIRIS Overview

## What is IIRIS?

IIRIS is a technology-driven risk, security, and intelligence consulting company born in India and trusted globally. The company helps organizations protect people, assets, reputation, and operations while navigating uncertainty with clarity, confidence, and resilience.

## What does IIRIS do?

IIRIS delivers end-to-end solutions across risk advisory, forensic investigations, security design, digital innovation, business continuity, command center strategy, specialized training, and resilience-building programs. The firm's approach combines advanced analytics, deep on-ground intelligence, and operational execution.

## What makes IIRIS different?

IIRIS goes beyond conventional consulting by combining intelligence-led assessments, customized technology-driven solutions, and practical execution support. The focus is not only on mitigating risk, but also on enabling faster decision-making, operational continuity, regulatory readiness, and long-term resilience.

## What sectors does IIRIS support?

IIRIS works with corporates, multinational companies, regulated industries, government entities, and sectors such as BFSI, healthcare, infrastructure, retail, manufacturing, technology, hospitality, and professional services.

## What makes IIRIS credible and trusted?

IIRIS is recognized through strong execution, a global operating footprint, respected industry associations, multiple certifications, and high client satisfaction. The company is known for practical, intelligence-led consulting that can be adapted to local realities across regions.

## Where does IIRIS operate?

IIRIS operates across India, the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, and partner-supported international markets. The company supports clients through regional offices, local expertise, and a wider global partner network.

## How can someone connect with IIRIS?

Organizations and individuals can connect with IIRIS through business enquiry channels, official social platforms, and the contact form on the IIRIS website.
