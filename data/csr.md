# IIRIS CSR

IIRIS CSR reflects the organization's commitment to creating meaningful impact beyond business operations. The initiative focuses on community welfare, education, mental health, senior care, humanitarian relief, environmental sustainability, and social resilience.

## Support for senior citizens and vulnerable communities

IIRIS supports senior citizens and vulnerable groups through partnerships with credible charitable institutions. This includes support for dignified living, care access, and social inclusion for people who need long-term assistance.

## Social welfare and humanitarian initiatives

The organization contributes to humanitarian causes, relief support, and community-focused programs that align with empathy, dignity, and long-term social value.

## Education, awareness, and resilience

IIRIS CSR also supports awareness-building, education-led impact, and resilience-oriented social programs designed to create practical and sustainable change.
