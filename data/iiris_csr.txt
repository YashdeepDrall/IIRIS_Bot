IIRIS CSR – Overview and Focus

IIRIS CSR represents the organisation’s commitment to creating meaningful impact beyond business operations. The initiative focuses on community welfare, education, mental health, senior care, environmental sustainability, and social resilience. Guided by empathy and responsibility, IIRIS CSR aims to build a safer, more equitable society while remaining aligned with the company’s core values and corporate ethos. Through structured and long-term initiatives, IIRIS integrates social responsibility into its identity, ensuring that growth is inclusive, ethical, and purpose-driven.

Support for Senior Citizens and Vulnerable Communities

IIRIS actively supports senior citizens and vulnerable populations through partnerships with credible charitable institutions. As an associated member of Sab Ka Rakha, a senior living initiative based in Nawanshahr, Punjab, IIRIS contributes to providing free and dignified living for nearly 250 senior citizens. The facility integrates modern technology to monitor medical, nutritional, and administrative needs, while fostering a mentally stimulating and socially engaging environment.
In addition, IIRIS supports The Earth Saviours Foundation, an organisation dedicated to sheltering abandoned elders, mentally disabled individuals, and survivors of abuse. This support helps ensure access to safe housing, medical care, rehabilitation, and long-term social inclusion, reinforcing the belief that caring for the vulnerable is a shared societal responsibility.

Social Welfare and Humanitarian Relief Initiatives

IIRIS undertakes multiple social welfare and humanitarian relief initiatives aimed at supporting underprivileged communities and responding to emergencies. Through its annual giving initiative, IIRIS has contributed significant financial support, including donations of ₹10 lakhs each to The Earth Saviours Foundation and the Dream Girl Foundation. These contributions support initiatives focused on community upliftment, environmental protection, and social development.
During extreme winter conditions in the Delhi NCR region, IIRIS partnered with Asha Bhawan NGO to conduct an emergency cold-wave relief drive. Employees contributed woollen clothing, which was distributed to vulnerable individuals through the NGO’s on-ground network. This initiative highlights the organisation’s commitment to collective action and timely humanitarian response.

Mental Health, Youth, and Education Support

IIRIS places strong emphasis on mental health, youth development, and education as essential pillars of societal well-being. The organisation supports adolescent mental health by creating safe and supportive spaces where teenagers can express themselves openly. Access to professional counselling and peer support programs helps young individuals build emotional resilience and coping skills.
IIRIS also encourages youth engagement in community service, involving children from the IIRIS family in volunteering activities at local NGOs. Rooted in the principle of Seva Bhav, these experiences promote empathy, responsibility, and intergenerational connection. School interactions and educational engagement further reinforce social awareness and ethical values among young minds.

CSR Philosophy and Importance

IIRIS believes that a truly secure and progressive society is built by caring for people beyond business outcomes. CSR is viewed not as an act of charity, but as a moral and social obligation toward elders, youth, vulnerable communities, and the environment. Through sustained partnerships, employee participation, and purpose-driven initiatives, IIRIS aims to create long-term, meaningful change. This philosophy reflects the organisation’s commitment to dignity, gratitude, humanity, and shared growth, ensuring that business success contributes positively to society as a whole.
