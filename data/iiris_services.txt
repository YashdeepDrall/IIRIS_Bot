What is Brand Management ?

Brand Management is the strategic process of building, maintaining, and enhancing a brand’s identity, perception, and value over time.

It focuses on how a brand is presented and experienced by customers, including brand positioning, messaging, visual identity, customer experience, reputation management, and consistency across all touchpoints. Brand management aims to create trust, loyalty, and emotional connection with audiences, ensuring the brand remains relevant and competitive in the market.

In simple terms:

Brand management = shaping and growing the brand’s image and value

Brand protection = defending the brand from misuse, fraud, and infringement
