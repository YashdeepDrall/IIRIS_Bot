# IIRIS Frequently Asked Questions

## What is IIRIS Consulting?

IIRIS Consulting is a global risk, security, and intelligence consulting firm that helps organizations protect people, assets, reputation, and operations across complex and changing environments.

## What kind of organizations does IIRIS support?

IIRIS works with corporates, multinational companies, regulated industries, public institutions, and organizations operating in high-risk or high-complexity sectors.

## What services does IIRIS provide?

IIRIS offers advisory and consultation, risk management, forensics and diligence, business continuity, emergency response, brand protection, security design, training, and related resilience services.

## Is IIRIS active globally?

Yes. IIRIS supports clients through offices in multiple regions, regional leadership presence, and a wider global partner network.

## Does IIRIS have leadership across multiple business units?

Yes. IIRIS has leadership across core operations, IntelliRisk, IntelliFuture, IIRIS Knowledge, and its Board of Advisory.

## How can I contact IIRIS?

IIRIS can be reached through business enquiries, HR and partnership channels, regional office contacts, and the official contact form on the website.

## What can this chatbot help with?

The chatbot is designed to answer questions about IIRIS, its leadership, services, locations, media coverage, press releases, official channels, and other supported knowledge-base topics.
