1. What is IIRIS Consulting?

IIRIS Consulting is a global risk, security, and intelligence consulting firm that helps organisations protect people, assets, reputation, and operations. IIRIS works across strategic advisory, risk management, investigations, business continuity, security design, training, and crisis response, enabling clients to operate confidently in uncertain environments.

2. What kind of companies does IIRIS work with?

IIRIS works with corporates, multinational companies, regulated industries, government bodies, and high-risk sectors such as BFSI, healthcare, manufacturing, infrastructure, retail, technology, and professional services. Its solutions are tailored to each organisation’s industry, risk profile, and regulatory environment.

3. What services does IIRIS provide?

IIRIS offers a comprehensive suite of services including Advisory & Consultation, Brand Protection, Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS), Emergency Response Services, Forensics & Diligence, Resource & Skills Provisioning, Risk Management, Security Design & Solutions, and specialised Training Programs. These services are designed to work independently or as an integrated risk and resilience ecosystem.

4. How is IIRIS different from traditional consulting or security firms?

IIRIS goes beyond off-the-shelf consulting. Its approach is intelligence-led, compliance-aligned, and execution-focused. With certified experts, ex-defence professionals, forensic specialists, and global partners, IIRIS not only advises but also works alongside client teams to implement, test, and sustain real-world solutions.

5. Does IIRIS operate only in India?

No. IIRIS has a strong global presence with offices in India, Singapore, UAE, Oman, Bahrain, and Nigeria. Through its international teams and partner network, IIRIS supports clients across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.

6. What kind of risks does IIRIS help manage?

IIRIS helps organisations manage a wide spectrum of risks including physical security threats, fraud and misconduct, cyber and digital risks, regulatory and compliance exposure, crisis situations, executive and travel risks, fire and life safety, workplace misconduct, mental health emergencies, and reputational threats.

7. Does IIRIS help during emergencies or crises?

Yes. IIRIS provides 24/7 Emergency Response Services, including quick response team deployment, medical emergency coordination, legal and ethical incident handling, whistleblower management, and crisis coordination with authorities. The focus is on rapid stabilisation, minimal disruption, and continuity of operations.

8. What is Business Continuity Management at IIRIS?

IIRIS designs and implements ISO 22301–aligned Business Continuity Management Systems that help organisations prepare for disruptions, recover faster, and maintain operations. This includes business impact analysis, threat assessments, continuity and disaster recovery planning, crisis simulations, audits, and employee training.

9. Does IIRIS handle investigations and forensic work?

Yes. IIRIS has a dedicated Forensics & Diligence practice covering white-collar crime investigations, forensic audits, due diligence, asset tracing, skip tracing, digital forensics, background verification, and structured investigative interviews using globally recognised techniques such as the Reid Method.

10. Can IIRIS help protect my brand and intellectual property?

Absolutely. IIRIS provides Brand Protection services to safeguard trademarks, patents, copyrights, and brand reputation. This includes counterfeit detection, market surveillance, enforcement actions, litigation support, trend intelligence, and AI-driven monitoring to combat brand misuse and IP violations.

11. Does IIRIS provide security system design and technology solutions?

Yes. IIRIS designs integrated security solutions including access control systems, surveillance and CCTV with AI analytics, intrusion detection, command and control centres, and unified security communication platforms. These solutions are built to be scalable, resilient, and aligned with business continuity goals.

12. Does IIRIS offer training programs?

IIRIS offers specialised training programs in leadership, corporate security, risk management, cybersecurity, POSH compliance, investigations, and industry-specific risk. It also offers advanced certifications such as the Certified Risk Leader program and the globally recognised REID Investigative Interviewing Certification.

13. Can IIRIS help with staffing and workforce needs?

Yes. Through Resource & Skills Provisioning, IIRIS supports contractual staffing, permanent hiring, industry-specific staffing, training, payroll support, and HR administrative services. All resources are pre-vetted, compliance-ready, and aligned with operational requirements.

14. Does IIRIS support ethical practices and whistleblowing?

Yes. IIRIS operates an independent whistleblower service that ensures secure, anonymous, and compliant reporting. It provides impartial case management, protection against retaliation, and support for internal investigations, helping organisations uphold ethics and trust.

15. Is IIRIS involved in social responsibility initiatives?

Yes. Through IIRIS CSR, the organisation actively supports senior care, mental health, education, humanitarian relief, environmental causes, and community development. CSR is viewed as a responsibility rooted in empathy, dignity, and long-term social impact—not charity.

16. Where is IIRIS headquartered?

IIRIS’s head office is located in Gurugram, Haryana, India, with multiple offices across major Indian cities and international locations to support global operations.
