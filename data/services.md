# IIRIS Services

## What services does IIRIS provide?

IIRIS provides a comprehensive suite of risk, security, and intelligence services including advisory and consultation, risk management, forensic investigations and diligence, brand protection, business continuity management, emergency response, security design and solutions, resource and skills provisioning, and specialized training programs.

## Advisory and Consultation

IIRIS delivers intelligence-driven guidance aligned with an organization's risk profile, regulatory environment, and operational realities. Advisory work includes enterprise risk management, crisis management planning, emergency response readiness, governance risk and compliance support, and security strategy across physical, digital, and operational environments.

## Risk Management

Risk management services are designed to protect people, assets, and operations in uncertain environments. They cover governance, compliance frameworks, fire safety audits, crisis management, security risk assessments, executive protection, alert and advisory services, and technical surveillance countermeasures.

## Forensics and Diligence

IIRIS supports forensic investigations, fraud reviews, due diligence, investigative fact-finding, legal coordination, and evidence-led reporting. These capabilities help organizations detect issues early, manage complex cases, and strengthen trust and accountability.

## Brand Protection

Brand protection services focus on intellectual property enforcement, anti-counterfeiting support, online and offline infringement response, investigative coordination, and legal support designed to protect reputation and business value.

## Security Design and Solutions

IIRIS supports security architecture, command center strategy, infrastructure security, surveillance planning, integrated security design, and technology-enabled protection frameworks for business-critical environments.

## Business Continuity and Emergency Response

IIRIS helps clients prepare for disruption through business continuity planning, continuity program design, crisis playbooks, emergency response frameworks, and resilience-focused operating models.

## Training and Capability Development

Training programs cover security awareness, resilience, operational readiness, leadership development, compliance-linked capability building, and sector-specific risk education.
